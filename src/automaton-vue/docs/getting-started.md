# Your first app

You need an Automaton server with a Runner and an AI agent that can use it. [Install with your AI agent](./install-with-ai) sets all of that up.

## What happens

1. **You describe the app.** Say what it should do, in your own words.
2. **Your AI builds it.** It creates the app from a template, writes the code, and uses the Automaton CLI to deploy it.
3. **Your server runs it.** Automaton builds the app and runs it on your Runner. A few minutes later it has its own address, such as `https://my-app.apps.yourdomain.com`.
4. **It is private at first.** Only you can open it until you share it or publish it. In Studio, **Who can open it?** controls this.
5. **Publish if you want.** Ask your AI to publish the app to your app store.

## Try it

> Build a small habit tracker with Automaton, deploy it to my server and tell me the address. Keep it private for now.

Later: *"Publish it to my app store."*
