## 3️⃣ Exactly When You Should Incorporate (Signals Checklist)

Create an **SL / LTD** when **ANY 2–3 of these are true**:

### Revenue Signals
- You charge subscriptions or credits
- You earn > €1,000–2,000 total
- Users expect invoices

### Usage & Risk Signals
- Apps have real users
- Users depend on uptime
- Apps process personal data seriously

### Operational Signals
- You collaborate with others
- You hire freelancers
- You need contracts

### Psychological Signal (Important)
- You feel anxious about personal liability

If multiple boxes are checked → **incorporate**.

## 4️⃣ SL vs Autónomo (Spain, Your Case)

### Autónomo (Self‑Employed)
**Pros**:
- Fast to start
- Low paperwork

**Cons**:
- Personal liability
- Monthly social security fees
- Poor long‑term scaling

### SL (Sociedad Limitada)
**Pros**:
- Limited liability
- Professional image
- Easier subscriptions & SaaS
- Cleaner accounting

**Cons**:
- Setup cost
- Accounting overhead

### Recommendation for You
| Phase | Best Option |
|----|----|
| Free / experimental | No entity |
| First revenue | SL |
| SaaS scaling | SL |

Skip autónomo unless legally required.
