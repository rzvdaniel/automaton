# Marketplace visibility and private runtimes

In Studio, choose **Publish** for an App and configure the two independent controls:

- **Runtime visibility** controls whether the App runtime can be opened outside Studio.
- **Marketplace visibility** controls whether the App appears in public marketplace discovery.

For a private App that is still intended for discovery, select **Private runtime** and **Listed in marketplace**. Its marketplace page shows its screenshots and other media, and can offer **Fork app** when remixing is enabled. It does not show an **Open app** action and displays **Private runtime — preview available through media.**

Existing published Apps remain public, listed, and launchable after the visibility migration. Unlisted Apps can still retain a public runtime when a direct URL is required, but they do not appear in marketplace discovery.
