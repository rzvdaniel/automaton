Automaton theme

One look for Studio, the website and every own app

Quiet paper and ink; orange only when something needs you or is happening; red only when something failed or will be destroyed. Never green, never blue.

Colours

Token names as in Studio's tokens.css. Switch dark mode at the top to see the dark values.

The mark

Files: resources/logo/ in automaton. Beside the app name in every header, 32 px.

Do

  • Paper cards on the panel background, 16 px radius, a 1 px line.
  • Keep last known values while refreshing; mark them, don't blank them.
  • History under its own item, newest first.
  • Check every screen in dark mode.

Don't

  • Green or blue anywhere, including "success".
  • Coloured left borders or tinted whole cards for status.
  • Colour without words.
  • New tokens without adding them here and to tokens.css.
